# KAALKRIT content and asset requests

The temporary public site is intentionally honest about what it does not yet have. The following inputs are required to turn it into a final sponsor-ready KAALKRIT package.

1. Final logo files: SVG, PNG, favicon, and brand guidelines.
2. Official team roster: names, roles, divisions, project ownership, short bios, and verified social links.
3. Real team photographs and workshop photographs.
4. Real project images and videos for each project.
5. Project captions, dates, status confirmation, and technical details approved for public use.
6. Competition name, date, and context for the 11th-place achievement.
7. Build With Hardware public, private, or concept status and an official URL if available.
8. Official institutional and legal information required for the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
9. Final deployment domain and `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.
10. Analytics decision and any resulting cookie-consent requirement.
11. Sponsorship narrative, collaboration options, and approved partner value proposition.
12. Social-profile imagery and a final public-communications owner.
13. Final legal review before broader public launch.

## Asset replacement notes
