About Team KAALKRIT

Team KAALKRIT is the official drone and robotics innovation team of Sir M. Visvesvaraya Institute of Technology (Sir MVIT), Bengaluru, established in 2024 with the vision of designing intelligent technologies that solve real-world challenges through innovation, research, and engineering excellence.

Our multidisciplinary team consists of passionate students from diverse engineering domains, working together to develop autonomous aerial systems, robotics, embedded technologies, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and advanced software solutions. By combining technical expertise with creativity and practical problem-solving, we transform ambitious ideas into impactful engineering innovations.

At Team KAALKRIT, innovation is more than building prototypes—it is about developing complete, reliable, and scalable solutions. Every project we undertake follows a comprehensive engineering process involving research, system architecture, design, manufacturing, electronics integration, software development, testing, validation, and continuous improvement.

Beyond competitions, our mission is to contribute to India's rapidly growing deep-tech ecosystem, inspire future innovators, promote indigenous technology development, and represent Sir MVIT with excellence on national and international platforms.

Our Projects

Autonomous Unmanned Aerial System (UAS) – NIDAR 2026

For NIDAR 2026, Team KAALKRIT successfully designed and developed a fully integrated Unmanned Aerial System (UAS), demonstrating expertise in airframe design, embedded electronics, flight controller integration, mission planning, payload management, and autonomous flight. This project laid a strong technical foundation for our future advancements in autonomous aerial technologies.



AirMOS – NIDAR 2027

Building upon our experience from UAS development, Team KAALKRIT is currently developing AirMOS for NIDAR 2027. AirMOS represents the next evolution of our research in intelligent aerial systems by integrating advanced sensing technologies, autonomous capabilities, and mission-oriented design to develop scalable drone solutions capable of addressing real-world challenges.



Build With Hardware (BWH)

Build With Hardware (BWH) is Team KAALKRIT's flagship engineering platform designed to make hardware innovation more accessible. The platform combines structured learning, practical hardware projects, technical documentation, collaborative workspaces, and AI-powered engineering assistance into a unified ecosystem, enabling students, developers, researchers, startups, and makers to learn, build, collaborate, and transform ideas into real-world hardware solutions.



Intelligent Robotic Arm

Our Intelligent Robotic Arm project demonstrates Team KAALKRIT's expertise in robotics, embedded systems, precision motion control, and intelligent automation. By integrating mechanical engineering, electronics, servo-based control, and advanced control algorithms, the system is being developed for future applications in industrial automation, smart manufacturing, precision handling, research, and autonomous robotic operations.



Autonomous Robot Vacuum Cleaner

Team KAALKRIT has also developed an Autonomous Robot Vacuum Cleaner, showcasing our capabilities in intelligent robotics, embedded systems, sensor integration, and autonomous navigation. Designed to perform efficient floor-cleaning operations with minimal human intervention, the system combines intelligent navigation, obstacle detection, motion control, and automation to demonstrate practical applications of robotics in everyday life.

This project reflects our commitment to building smart, user-centric robotic solutions while strengthening our expertise in autonomous mobility, embedded electronics, and real-world product development.
